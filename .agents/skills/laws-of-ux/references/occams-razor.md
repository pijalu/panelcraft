# Occam’s Razor

## Concise explanation

Among equally effective explanations or designs, prefer the simpler one. Simplicity means
fewer unnecessary assumptions and interactions, not removal of needed functionality.

## PanelCraft application

Prefer direct editing, existing commands, shared panel behavior, and clear defaults over
parallel workflows or special cases. Keep advanced model capabilities available when needed.

## Common failure modes

- Adding a new mode when a small extension to an existing flow would work.
- Removing essential controls or information in the name of minimalism.
- Simplifying visuals while increasing implementation or state complexity.

## Review questions

- Which step, state, or concept can be removed without harming the task?
- Is the simpler alternative equally capable and accessible?
