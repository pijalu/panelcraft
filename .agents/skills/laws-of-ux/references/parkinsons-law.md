# Parkinson’s Law

## Concise explanation

Work may expand to fill the time available. In UX, use the idea to avoid unnecessary waiting,
procedural detours, and scope growth—not to pressure users or impose arbitrary deadlines.

## PanelCraft application

Keep routine edits direct, avoid artificial delays, support efficient repeated operations,
and time-box design exploration around a clear user outcome and measurable acceptance test.

## Common failure modes

- Adding confirmation or animation delays to low-risk repeated actions.
- Rushing users through timed tasks or auto-advancing without control.
- Cutting essential validation merely to shorten a workflow.

## Review questions

- Which delays or steps have no user or safety benefit?
- Does efficiency preserve user control, accuracy, and accessibility?
