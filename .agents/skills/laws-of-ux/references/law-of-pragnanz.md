# Law of Prägnanz

## Concise explanation

People tend to perceive complex images in simple, orderly forms. Clear silhouettes and regular
structure reduce ambiguity, especially in dense visual information.

## PanelCraft application

Use recognizable shape geometry, consistent routes, stable alignment, and restrained canvas
decoration. Let architecture semantics—not ornamental complexity—drive visual structure.

## Common failure modes

- Oversimplifying shapes until different object kinds become indistinguishable.
- Allowing labels, glyphs, markers, and selection cues to form visual clutter.
- Using symmetry that misrepresents actual relationships.

## Review questions

- Can users distinguish objects, links, and overlays at a glance?
- Does simplification preserve essential meaning and accessibility?
