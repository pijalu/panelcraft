# Jakob’s Law

## Concise explanation

Users bring expectations from other products. Familiar conventions lower learning cost,
while deviations need a clear benefit and adequate explanation.

## PanelCraft application

Follow familiar file, clipboard, tab, panel, shortcut, and dialog conventions. Align canvas
editing with established diagram-tool behavior while preserving PanelCraft's code-first model.

## Common failure modes

- Inventing unfamiliar names or gestures for standard operations.
- Copying conventions that conflict with browser-local storage or accessibility.
- Assuming familiarity excuses missing labels or feedback.

## Review questions

- Which established pattern does this interaction resemble?
- If it differs, is the benefit clear and the behavior learnable?
