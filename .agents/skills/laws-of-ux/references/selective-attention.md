# Selective Attention

## Concise explanation

Attention is selective; people focused on a task may miss unrelated changes, even visually
prominent ones. Important feedback should appear in a meaningful context and modality.

## PanelCraft application

Place validation and save feedback near the editing context, highlight affected canvas/code
participants, and avoid relying on a transient peripheral notification for critical problems.

## Common failure modes

- Assuming a banner or color flash will always be noticed.
- Letting several animations and badges compete for attention.
- Moving focus unexpectedly just to make feedback visible.

## Review questions

- Will users notice essential feedback while focused on their primary task?
- Is persistent, accessible feedback available after transient emphasis ends?
