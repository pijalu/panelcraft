# Goal-Gradient Effect

## Concise explanation

Visible proximity to completion can increase motivation. Progress information is useful when
it reflects real advancement and helps users decide what remains.

## PanelCraft application

Expose truthful save, import, export, and multi-step flow progress; preserve unsaved state;
make remaining errors or required fields clear without manufacturing completion indicators.

## Common failure modes

- Showing indeterminate or fake progress as if it were measurable.
- Celebrating progress while hiding a final destructive or irreversible step.
- Adding steps solely to make progress appear more substantial.

## Review questions

- Is progress based on real, understandable milestones?
- Can users tell what remains and safely pause or recover?
