# Peak-End Rule

## Concise explanation

Recall of an experience is influenced strongly by intense moments and its ending rather than
by a simple average. Critical failures and final feedback deserve particular attention.

## PanelCraft application

Make save/export completion, destructive recovery, import errors, and flow playback endings
clear and trustworthy. Ensure stopped or completed animations settle into coherent states.

## Common failure modes

- Polishing routine screens while leaving error or completion states broken.
- Leaving orphan tokens, stale diagnostics, or ambiguous unsaved state at the end.
- Using celebratory feedback to obscure partial failure.

## Review questions

- What are the most emotionally significant moments in this workflow?
- Is the final state explicit, settled, and recoverable?
