# Law of Uniform Connectedness

## Concise explanation

Visually connected elements are perceived as related, often more strongly than elements linked
only by proximity or similarity. Connections must therefore represent real relationships.

## PanelCraft application

Make connector paths, endpoint attachment, flow highlighting, and group ownership visually
explicit. Avoid decorative lines that could be mistaken for architecture relationships.

## Common failure modes

- Routes that appear attached to the wrong object or cross ambiguously.
- Selection guides that resemble persisted connectors.
- Connected visuals without equivalent textual or semantic descriptions.

## Review questions

- Does every visible connection communicate an actual relationship?
- Are endpoints and selected routes clear in both themes and at different zoom levels?
