# Law of Proximity

## Concise explanation

Nearby elements are perceived as related. Deliberate spacing can establish hierarchy and
relationships even without lines or containers.

## PanelCraft application

Keep labels close to their controls, connector labels close to routes, and panel actions near
the content they affect. Use consistent token-based gaps to separate unrelated groups.

## Common failure modes

- Ambiguous label ownership caused by equal spacing.
- Placing destructive actions close to frequent safe actions.
- Depending on proximity when reading order or semantics disagree.

## Review questions

- Is every label and action unambiguously associated with its target?
- Does spacing follow the style guide and remain clear at narrow widths?
