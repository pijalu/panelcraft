# Pareto Principle

## Concise explanation

A relatively small set of causes or features often accounts for a large share of effects or
use. This heuristic guides prioritization; actual proportions must come from evidence.

## PanelCraft application

Prioritize reliable create/edit/connect/save/export workflows and common diagnostics before
rare refinements, while maintaining access to specialized modeling features.

## Common failure modes

- Assuming an 80/20 ratio without task or usage evidence.
- Ignoring accessibility or data safety because they affect fewer sessions.
- Removing low-frequency but high-value recovery and export functions.

## Review questions

- Which workflows have the greatest user value or failure impact?
- Do prioritization decisions protect minority needs and critical paths?
