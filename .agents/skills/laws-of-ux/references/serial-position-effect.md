# Serial Position Effect

## Concise explanation

Items near the beginning and end of a sequence are often remembered more readily than middle
items. Ordering can aid recall but should primarily support the task and meaning.

## PanelCraft application

Place primary and recovery actions deliberately in menus, keep flow step order explicit, and
summarize important diagnostics rather than depending on users remembering a long list.

## Common failure modes

- Burying essential commands among many similar items.
- Reordering semantic flow steps merely for visual emphasis.
- Assuming list position substitutes for labels or search.

## Review questions

- Does ordering support priority, chronology, and user expectations?
- Are middle items still discoverable and operable?
